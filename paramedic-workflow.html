EMS 221

Paramedic Cardiovascular Care

Lecture
3 CreditsSpring

Description

Covers in-depth assessment and management of cardiovascular conditions. Includes ALS airway management, electrical therapy, pharmacology, and drug/fluid administration with emphasis on patient assessment, differential diagnosis, and management of cardiovascular emergencies.

Key Topics

ECG InterpretationDysrhythmiasACS ManagementHeart FailureCardiac Arrest

Program Requirements

Ensure you meet all prerequisites before starting

Prerequisites

  • Current Virginia AEMT certification (or NREMT with VA reciprocity)
  • Current CPR certification (AHA BLS Provider)
  • Admission to Paul D. Camp Community College
  • Completed health requirements
  • Background check clearance
  • Active Sterling Credentials account

Health Requirements

  • Physical Exam (within 1 year)
  • TB Screening (QuantiFERON Gold)
  • Varicella vaccination
  • MMR vaccination
  • TDAP (within 10 years)
  • Hepatitis B series
  • Annual Influenza vaccine
