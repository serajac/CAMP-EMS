Program Requirements

Prerequisites

  • Current Virginia AEMT certification
  • Current CPR certification (AHA BLS)
  • Admission to CAMP EMS Program
  • Background check clearance
  • Active Sterling Credentials account

Health Requirements

  • Physical Exam (within 1 year)
  • TB Screening (QuantiFERON Gold)
  • Varicella, MMR, TDAP vaccinations
  • Hepatitis B series
  • Annual Influenza vaccine
