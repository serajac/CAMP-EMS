Program Overview

You Made the Right Choice

Congratulations on taking the first step toward becoming a paramedic. By choosing Camp Community College's XPM Program, you've joined an elite group of dedicated individuals committed to excellence in emergency medical care.

Our accelerated program isn't just about earning a certification—it's about transforming lives, including your own. Every day, paramedics make split-second decisions that save lives. We're here to ensure you're ready for that responsibility.

Why Camp Community College?

Virginia's Only Dual LOR

We're the only program in Virginia with Letters of Review from CoAEMSP for both AEMT and Paramedic programs.

Norfolk Fire & Rescue Partnership

Gain real-world experience with one of Virginia's busiest fire departments during your clinical rotations.

Expert Faculty

Learn from experienced EMS professionals who bring decades of field experience into the classroom.

Multiple Certifications

Graduate with ACLS, PALS, PHTLS, and AMLS certifications—ready to excel in any EMS environment.

Our Commitment to You

"At Camp Community College, we don't just train paramedics—we develop healthcare leaders. Our mission is to provide you with the cognitive knowledge, psychomotor skills, and professional attitudes necessary to become an exceptional emergency medical professional. When you graduate from our program, you won't just be competent—you'll be confident, compassionate, and ready to make a difference in your community."

— Dr. Carlos Cajares, Program Director

Program Structure

1

Spring Semester (Feb 9 – May 8)

2

Summer Clinical Phase (May 11 – July 17)

Field rotations with Norfolk Fire & Rescue. Progress from supervised patient care to team leader with full autonomy in your capstone internship.

3

Campus Return & Certifications (July 20 – Aug 1)

Return to campus for PALS, PHTLS, AMLS certifications, comprehensive review, and National Registry exam preparation.

Courses

Click on any course to view detailed information including objectives and major topics.

Spring 2026 17 Credits

EMS 210

EMS Operations

1 Credit Online

Focuses on EMS operations, incident and scene safety, triage, MCI operations, and medical incident management. Develops awareness of principles, techniques, and procedures for safe emergency scene management.

Course Objectives:
  • • Describe ground and air operations components
  • • Explain triage principles (START, JumpSTART, SALT)
  • • Describe ICS structure and operations
  • • Discuss MCI management principles
  • • Integrate hazmat incident management
  • • Apply traffic incident management principles
Major Topics:
  • • ICS Structure
  • • MCI Management
  • • Triage Systems
  • • Hazmat Awareness
  • • Rescue Operations
  • • Crime Scene Awareness
  • • Air Medical Operations
EMS 212

Leadership and Professional Development

1 Credit Online

Develops leadership within EMS including civic engagement, personal wellness, resource management, and ethical considerations. Prepares responders for leadership roles in their agency and community.

Course Objectives:
  • • Discuss paramedic role in public health
  • • Describe components of professionalism
  • • Propose community paramedicine initiatives
  • • List injury prevention strategies
  • • Discuss stress effects and prevention
  • • Apply ethical considerations in leadership
Major Topics:
  • • Public Health Role
  • • Professionalism
  • • Community Paramedicine
  • • Injury Prevention
  • • Stress Management
  • • Ethics in Leadership
  • • Career Development
EMS 221 / EMS 222

Paramedic Cardiovascular Care (Lecture & Lab)

3 Credits Hybrid

Covers in-depth assessment and management of cardiovascular conditions as outlined by NEMSES. Includes advanced airway management, electrical therapy, pharmacology, and emphasis on patient assessment and differential diagnosis.

Course Objectives:
  • • Integrate cardiovascular A&P knowledge
  • • Integrate scene safety awareness
  • • Apply ECG interpretation principles
  • • Demonstrate defibrillation proficiency
  • • Perform synchronized cardioversion
  • • Acquire and interpret 12-lead ECGs
  • • Manage cardiac arrest per AHA guidelines
Major Topics:
  • • Cardiovascular A&P
  • • ECG Interpretation
  • • 12-Lead ECG Acquisition
  • • Defibrillation & Cardioversion
  • • Transcutaneous Pacing
  • • Cardiac Arrest Management
  • • ACS Recognition & Treatment
  • • Dysrhythmia Recognition
EMS 223 / EMS 224

Paramedic Patient Care I (Lecture & Lab)

5 Credits Lecture/Lab

Covers the breadth of medical and trauma conditions as outlined by NEMSES. Part I of II. Equips students with knowledge and skills for effective patient care across the lifespan.

Medical Emergencies:
  • • Abdominal/GI disorders
  • • EENT disorders
  • • Endocrine disorders
  • • Neurologic disorders
  • • Immunologic disorders
  • • Infectious disease
  • • Toxicology
  • • Psychiatric emergencies
Trauma Emergencies:
  • • Bleeding and fluid resuscitation
  • • Chest trauma
  • • Head/facial/neck/spine trauma
  • • Orthopedic trauma
  • • Soft tissue trauma
  • • Multi-system trauma
  • • Environmental emergencies
EMS 225 / EMS 226

Paramedic Patient Care II (Lecture & Lab)

7 Credits Lecture/Lab

Covers the depth of medical and trauma conditions as outlined by NEMSES. Part II of II. Continues emphasis on patient assessment, differential diagnosis, and advanced treatment modalities.

In-Depth Medical:
  • • Obstetric emergencies
  • • Neonatal resuscitation
  • • Pediatric medical emergencies
  • • Geriatric considerations
  • • Psychiatric crisis intervention
  • • Toxicology/overdose/withdrawal
  • • Hematologic disorders
  • • Infectious disease/sepsis
In-Depth Trauma & Skills:
  • • ATLS principles
  • • Damage control resuscitation
  • • TBI management
  • • Thoracic trauma
  • • Burns (thermal, chemical, electrical)
  • • Surgical cricothyrotomy
  • • Needle/finger thoracostomy
  • • Team leader scenarios

Summer 2026 16 Credits

BIO 141

Human Anatomy and Physiology I

4 Credits Online

Presents study of anatomy and physiology including anatomical terminology, homeostasis, histology, integumentary, skeletal, muscular, and nervous systems. Part I of II.

Course Objectives:
  • • Define anatomy and physiology interrelationship
  • • Apply anatomical and directional terminology
  • • Describe homeostasis and feedback mechanisms
  • • Describe tissue structure and functions
  • • Explain skeletal system in calcium regulation
  • • Describe muscle contraction and relaxation
Major Topics:
  • • Anatomical Terminology
  • • Homeostasis & Feedback
  • • Histology & Tissue Types
  • • Integumentary System
  • • Skeletal System
  • • Muscular System
  • • Nervous System
  • • Autonomic Nervous System

  • • Navigate college policies

  • • Create academic plan
Major Topics:
  • • Learning Styles

  • • Career Planning
  • • College Resources
EMS 163 / 164 / 165 / 169

Certification Courses (PHTLS, AMLS, ACLS, PALS)

4 Credits Hybrid

National certification courses that prepare you for the highest level of patient care in trauma, medical, cardiac, and pediatric emergencies.

PHTLS (EMS 163):
  • • Kinematics of Trauma
  • • Rapid Trauma Assessment
  • • Hemorrhage Control
  • • Spinal Motion Restriction
AMLS (EMS 164):
  • • Assessment-Based Approach
  • • Medical Differential Diagnosis
  • • Clinical Reasoning
ACLS (EMS 165):
  • • Cardiac Arrest Algorithms
  • • Bradycardia/Tachycardia Management
  • • ACS & Stroke Protocols
PALS (EMS 169):
  • • Pediatric Assessment Triangle
  • • Pediatric Resuscitation
  • • Pediatric Pharmacology
EMS 241 / 246 / 249

Clinical Internships I, II & Capstone

6 Credits Clinical

Progressive clinical rotations with Norfolk Fire & Rescue. Develop from supervised student to team leader with full autonomy.

EMS 241 - Internship I:
  • • Supervised patient care
  • • Documentation practice
  • • Protocol application
  • • Skills performance
EMS 246 - Internship II:
  • • Progressive autonomy
  • • Team leader development
  • • Critical thinking
  • • Advanced skills
EMS 249 - Capstone:
  • • Full team leader role
  • • Minimum 20 patients
  • • Entry-level competency
  • • Final evaluation
EMS 216

Paramedic Review

1 Credit Lab

Intensive review for the practical and written portions of the National Registry Paramedic exam. Prepares students for cognitive and psychomotor testing.

Cognitive Review:
  • • Airway management
  • • Trauma assessment
  • • Medical emergencies
  • • OB/GYN emergencies
  • • Pediatric emergencies
  • • EMS Operations
Psychomotor Practice:
  • • Medical Assessment
  • • Trauma Assessment
  • • Dynamic Cardiology
  • • Static Cardiology
  • • IV/IO Access
  • • Integrated scenarios
